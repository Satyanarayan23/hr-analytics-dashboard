
# HR Analytics Dashboard

## What Are We Trying to Solve?

Organizations need to understand their workforce, monitor employee attrition, and identify factors that influence employee satisfaction and retention.

This HR Analytics project analyzes employee data to explore workforce distribution, attrition patterns, compensation, work-life balance, job satisfaction, and other HR-related metrics. The dashboard helps users understand workforce trends and identify areas that may require attention.

## What the Dashboard Shows

### 1. Workforce Overview
Presents key HR metrics, including total employees, active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, and average employee age.

### 2. Employee Demographics
Explores workforce distribution across gender and other employee-related categories.

### 3. Employee Attrition Analysis
Examines attrition patterns across departments and explores relationships with monthly income, distance from home, and years since last promotion.

### 4. Job Role and Work-Life Balance
Analyzes employee distribution across job roles and work-life balance ratings.

### 5. Job Satisfaction Analysis
Compares average job satisfaction across departments and job roles.

### 6. Educational Background
Explores employee distribution across different educational fields.
